## 问题出在哪

35 张的毛病归下来是五类，都能在原提示词里找到原因：

| 毛病 | 几张 | 原因 | 这次怎么改 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 全身图、不是标准半身 | 17 | 姿势写了跪、坐、蹲、跑、跳、攀、骑、站在车上，GPT 一看就把腿画出来，第二轮加了「只画到腰」也压不住 | 除顾雍外一律**站姿**，动作全放在腰以上；地上的道具（溪水、车、石头、地上画的图）删掉 |
| 手不对、三只手、少一只手、刀没握住 | 8 | 一只手同时干两件事（扶膝又指路、托头盔又捧刀），或者干的事太复杂 | 每人单列**左手、右手**各干一件事，动作尽量简单；第一条消息里加一条「画完数手」 |
| 像骷髅、畸形小儿、弱智、肥婆 | 5 | 体型词写过头：「瘦得只剩骨头」「大头小身子」「孩子一样光滑」「骨架很重」 | 删掉这些词，写明正常成年人比例、脸上有肉；该丑的人只丑在五官，不动比例 |
| 白边、人偏 | 4 | 白边是 GPT 自己加的边框；人偏是桌子、城墙这些景占了半边 | 第一条消息加「检查四条边」；人偏的写明居中、景退到后面 |
| 道具画坏 | 3 | 阎行是我原提示词写了「枪杆断在枪头后面」，照着画的；关银屏没写弓多大；张辽没写刀多大 | 改正，写明尺寸 |

另外两处要你知道：

- **马良的白眉是典故**（「马氏五常，白眉最良」，眉中有白毛）。原提示词写成了整条白眉，跟年轻的脸放在一起很怪。这次改成黑眉，眉心夹一撮白毛，看得出又不扎眼。你要彻底不要白眉，把 FACE 里那半句删掉就行。
- **曹节**：游戏里她是曹操的女儿、献帝的皇后（列传和技能「掷玺」都是她），所以这次还画女的，但改成清瘦端正的年轻皇后，不再是粗骨架、张嘴骂人。你觉得「该是男的」，多半是因为羁绊「宦官」（张让、蹇硕、曹节、黄皓、岑昏）把她算进去了，那是十常侍里同名的宦官曹节，数据串了。这条羁绊我回头把她换掉，你要是想换成别人告诉我，不说我就换成同为十常侍的**赵忠**（游戏里没有他的话就改成四人羁绊）。
- 你写的「马忠」我按**马忠（吴）**处理：蜀的马忠是半身，吴的那个蹲在芦苇里，是全身。

## 怎么用

1. 开新对话。第一条消息先上传 3 张参考图：`assets/立绘/无双/赵云.png`、`assets/立绘/虎/徐晃.png`、`assets/立绘/虎/田丰.png`。这三张取景、纸色、人物大小都是标准的。
2. 贴下面「第一条消息」整段，等它回 ready。
3. 按顺序贴各批，每批 3 张。出完 3、4 批开新对话，重传参考图、重贴第一条消息。
4. 每张出来先看四样：**有没有腿**、**数手是不是两只**、**四条边有没有白条**、**人是不是在正中**。有一样不对就当场让它重画，别存。
5. 存成批里写的路径（中文名），覆盖旧图。

```
You are painting character portraits for a Chinese Three Kingdoms strategy game. I attached 3 reference images. Every new image must look like it belongs to the SAME SET as the references. The rules below beat anything in a character description.

- Background is light, simple and sparse, lighter than the figure, with white paper showing.

2. FRAMING LOCK
- Canvas exactly 3:4 portrait (e.g. 1086x1448).
- FULL BLEED: paint all the way to all four edges. No white strip, no margin, no border, no frame, no mount on any side.
- WAIST-UP ONLY, cropped at the hips, exactly like the references. Legs, knees and feet never appear.
- The figure is BIG: head plus torso fill 75–90% of the image height. Top of the head or helmet 4–8% below the top edge.
- The figure is CENTERED: the face is in the middle third of the width. Scenery stays behind the figure, never pushes it to one side.
- One figure only, three-quarter view.
- Both hands and everything held stay inside the frame, at least 6% from the left and right edges.

3. STANCE LOCK
- Everyone STANDS upright, unless the description says "seated". Never kneeling, crouching, squatting, sitting on the ground, running, leaping, climbing, riding, or standing on a cart or wall. A seated figure is cropped at the lap.

4. BODY LOCK
- Normal healthy adult proportions, like the references: head about one seventh of body height, shoulders as wide as two heads.
- Faces have real flesh over the bones. No skeletal, emaciated or corpse-like faces, no sunken eye sockets. No oversized head, no child proportions. No comic, goofy or silly faces. An ugly character is ugly in his features only, never in his proportions.

5. HANDS LOCK
- Each description lists LEFT HAND and RIGHT HAND. Draw exactly those two hands, each doing only that one thing. No third hand, no hand hidden for no reason.
- Five fingers per hand. A hand holding something visibly wraps its fingers around it.
- Spear and polearm shafts are one straight continuous piece running through the hand. Bows have exactly one string attached to both tips.

6. STYLE TEXT (apply to every image)

Face modeled with the traditional "three whites" (forehead, nose bridge, chin) and a touch of pink on the cheeks; no western shadow. The face must be a specific East Asian adult with real bone structure, drawn to the description, not a generic handsome face.
COMPOSITION: single figure, waist-up, three-quarter view, centered, head in the upper third, vertical 3:4 canvas, cropped at the hips. Background in the same flat gongbi manner, simplified, lighter than the figure, never overlapping the face.
FINISH: fine paper texture, colors flat and rich. No text, no seal, no signature, no border, no frame.

NEGATIVE: aged paper, yellowed silk, sepia, muted, ink wash, loose brushwork, anime, manga, chibi, big eyes, beauty-filter face, generic handsome man, generic pretty woman, western features, photorealistic, 3D render, glossy skin, chiaroscuro, multiple figures, text, watermark, signature, white border, margin, frame, beige paper, parchment, oil painting, full body, legs, knees, feet, shoes, kneeling, crouching, sitting on the ground, running, small figure, off-center figure, three hands, extra arm, missing hand, broken spear shaft, skeletal face, emaciated, big head, child proportions, goofy face.

7. OUTPUT PROTOCOL
- I send batches of 3 characters. Make exactly 3 SEPARATE images, in the order given, never a sheet or grid.
- Before showing each image, check: (a) no legs or feet; (b) count the hands — exactly two; (c) all four edges painted, no white strip; (d) face in the middle third; (e) same paper and saturation as the references. If any check fails, redraw.

Reply "ready" and wait for the first batch.
```
